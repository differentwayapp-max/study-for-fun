# 📖 User guide – Study for fun

## 👋 What is Study for fun

Study for fun is an app where kids practise school material through games – for a test, for review or to strengthen what they learn.

The parent picks or builds the practice, and the kid plays. The parent sees the progress and what to strengthen.

**Subjects:** Math, Hebrew language, Science and English.

**Grades:** 1–8.

## 🔑 Signing in

### Parent

- Sign in with a Google account.
- The family is created automatically, and whoever opened it is the family manager.

### Kid

- The kid joins with a **personal link** the parent sends. No password, no email needed.
- Open the link once on the kid's phone. From then on the app opens straight on that kid.
- It's a good idea to add the app to the phone's home screen, to open it from an icon.

## ☰ Side menu

Tap ☰ to open the menu. Your name and family are at the top, and below them the items, grouped by topic:

- **Family & practice** – **Family & adults** (the kids, their personal links and the adults in the family) and **Practice catalog** (ready practice sets by grade and subject).
- **Help & support** – **User guide** (this guide) and **Report a problem or an idea** (send a note to the app team).
- **Settings** – **Language**: switch between Hebrew and English. The questions themselves are not translated.
- **Account & privacy** – **Privacy policy**, **Terms of use**, **Sign out** and **Delete account**.

## 👧 Adding a kid and sending the link

### Adding a kid

- On the kids screen, go to the **＋ Kid** card.
- Type a first name, choose a grade and a colour, and tap **Save**.

### Sending the personal link

- On the kid's card, tap 🔗.
- Send it on WhatsApp or copy the link.
- Each kid has their own link, and it saves progress for that kid only.

## 📚 Practice from the catalog

- Open the **Practice catalog**.
- Choose a grade → a subject.
- Tick ✓ the topics of the test.
- Choose who the practice is for, and add a test date if you like (optional).
- Tap **Add**.

When a catalog set is updated, the kid's copy updates by itself and progress is kept.

## ✨ Building a practice set with AI

You can build a practice set from the test material itself.

- At the bottom of the catalog, tap **Build from your own material**.
- Choose a kid and a subject, and add a test date if there is one.
- Write the topics, one per line.
- Upload a photo of worksheets or a PDF file.
- Tap **Build the practice set**.

Building takes about 5–15 minutes. Wait only until the material is sent (a few seconds); then you can close the app and turn the screen off – the build goes on, and the practice set will appear on the kid's card when it is ready.

**Limit:** up to 3 new practice sets a month per family. Adding from the catalog is not limited.

## ⚙️ Practice set settings

Tap a practice set on the kid's card. Some of the options are in the ⋯ menu at the top of the practice set:

- **🎮 Game types on the road** – the app picks a suitable game for each station. You can remove types that don't suit the kid.
- **✏️ Edit practice set** – add questions from worksheets, add a chapter or remove one.
- **💌 Invite friends to the app** – send the practice set to other parents. They get a copy for their kids and don't see yours.
- **📤 Suggest for the catalog** – send the set for review, to be added to the catalog.
- **✅ Answer check** – automatically checks the answers in the set.
- **🗑 Delete practice set**

The catalog also has a **💌 Invite friends with this practice set** button.

## 🗺️ What the kid sees

### The map

- Each chapter is a station on the map.
- ⭐ on a station = the kid passed the chapter with 70% or more.
- A chapter not passed stays open for another try.
- With a test date, the map spreads the chapters until the test and shows a daily goal.

### The games

- **Multiple choice** – pick the right answer out of 4.
- **Match the lines** – draw a line between a question and its answer.
- **🧩 Riddles** – build the answer from letters, with a 💡 hint when stuck.
- **Letter order** – put the letters of the answer in order (in English you also hear the word).

Riddles and letter order are writing games, so they appear at one station only on the road – in a chapter whose answers are short words. When that station is played again, they take turns.
- **🎁 Memory** – a bonus. It doesn't count toward progress.
- **📖 Reading passage** – in reading chapters you read a passage and answer questions about it. Tap 🔊 to hear the passage, pause ⏸ and go on from the same word. The word being read is highlighted, and tapping a word starts reading from there.

During a game there is read aloud of the question 🔊, "💡 Why?" with an explanation after answering, and 🚩 to report a mistake in a question.

### Rewards

- ⭐ A star for every chapter passed with 70% or more.
- 🏆 Daily trophy and 🔥 day streak.

### "What I know" 📊

A screen for the kid that shows chapters to try again, words and questions to practise again, and what they already know. The **🎯 Practise them now** button opens a game on the mistakes only.

## 📊 Progress for the parent

On the feedback screen:

- Choose a period, a kid and a subject.
- See rounds completed, questions answered, success rate and days with practice.
- **🔎 See the mistakes** – every question the kid got wrong, and what they answered instead.
- **Success by topic** – Strengthen (under 60%), Almost there (60%–80%), Mastered (80% or more). A status is set only with at least 3 questions in the topic.

### 🎯 Focused practice

The app builds a practice set from your kid's mistakes only – free, in one tap.

- **Where:** on the practice set's card in the progress screen, and at the top of the kid's home (on a parent's phone) – when a set is done, or when an open set has collected mistakes.
- **One button:**
  - There are words or questions the kid got wrong – **🎯 Focused practice** is ready at once, with no AI, and doesn't use a monthly build.
  - There are only topics to strengthen – **✨ Focused practice for the topics** builds a new set with AI on those topics, counted in the month's builds.
- The focused set is marked 🎯 and comes first for the kid. The parent sees what's already fixed ("Fixed: … ✓").
- The original set moves to "✓ Finished practice sets" marked "🎯 moved to focused practice". When the focused set is done too, a new one can be built from what's left.
- "Later" hides the card for every adult in the family. New mistakes bring it back.

### Finished practice sets

A finished practice set moves to a separate section, **✓ Finished practice sets**, below the active ones.

## 👨‍👩‍👧 Family & adults

- You can invite another adult: a partner, a grandparent, a private tutor.
- Roles: manager (everything), helper (sees progress and adds practice sets), tutor (chosen kids only).
- The invitation is sent on WhatsApp or copied, and is valid for 7 days.

## 🏅 Competition between the kids

In the practice set settings you can turn on **Competition between the kids**. Kids collect points for rounds and trophies, and see a leaderboard.

## 🛠️ Common problems

- **"This phone is not connected to a kid yet"** – open the personal link from the kid's card on the kid's phone.
- **"This link is not active"** – the parent sends the link again from the kid's card.
- **"Can't reach your practice sets"** – check the internet connection and try again.
- **iPhone with Lockdown Mode** – in Safari: aA → Website Settings → turn off Lockdown Mode for this site.
- **A build failed** – delete it on the card and try again.
- **A problem or an idea** – ☰ → **Report a problem or an idea**.
