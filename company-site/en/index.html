Why D-Way

Built for families, out of a real need

Over the past decade, and in Israel in particular, the challenge facing families who must balance home and work has intensified in the face of global crises.

At D-Way, out of this reality, we have built a set of computerized tools that connect naturally to the needs and the ways of working of the new world.

Vision

D-Way's vision is to turn family time into quality time, with more positive energy, more cooperation and less friction.

The way there: solutions based on automation and gamification tools for managing family life simply and effectively.

Shared responsibility

Every member of the household knows what they are responsible for, and the load does not rest on one person.

Change in a positive spirit

Implementing and accompanying the change process in a positive spirit, with the help of gamification tools.

Automation

Reminders, tracking and coordination happen automatically, freeing parents from constant follow-up.

Company

Methods and tools from organizational development for businesses, adapted to the home

D-Way is an Israeli company that develops, operates and markets digital solutions for family, parenting and leisure. The solutions are adapted to the home and to family life, and rely on advanced systems that streamline the process and deliver convenience and simplicity.

How

We give the family collaborative tools that provide an accurate, shared picture and give every member of the household control suited to their age and level of responsibility, in an interactive and engaging way.

Empowerment and responsibility

Empowering every partner in the household and helping each of them take responsibility.

Process implementation

Family decisions that become routine and hold over time.

Gamification

Game mechanisms that encourage persistence and the involvement of children and parents.

Change leadership

A change in habits through a collaborative process that the whole family takes part in.
