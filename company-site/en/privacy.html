Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

How D-Way Family Lab uses the information provided on d-way.app.

1. Who we are

The website d-way.app is operated by D-Way Family Lab ("D-Way").

For any privacy question: differentway.app@gmail.com.

2. What information we collect

Only information you choose to provide in the contact form: full name, email address and message, and, if you wish, organization or company, phone and subject.

You are not legally required to provide this information, but without a name, an email address and a message we cannot get back to you.

3. Why we use the information

To reply to your inquiry and to schedule a meeting. We will not send you marketing emails without your consent, and we will not sell the information or pass it on to others, except for the services in section 4.

4. Outside services that process the information

  • Web3Forms – delivers the form details to our mailbox.
  • Google (Gmail) – the mailbox where inquiries are kept.
  • Google Fonts – the fonts on the site load from Google servers, so your browser sends its IP address to Google.
  • Google (Firebase Hosting) – the server that delivers the site and logs IP addresses as part of normal operation.

Some of these services operate outside Israel.

5. Cookies and tracking

The site does not use cookies, and does not use tracking, advertising or analytics tools.

6. How long we keep the information

For as long as it is needed to handle your inquiry and to stay in touch with you. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

7. Your rights

You may review your information, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to us at differentway.app@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the updated version will be published on this page, with the date of the update.
