Accessibility statement

Last updated: November 21, 2026

D-Way considers it important that the site is accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities.

Accessibility level

The site was built according to the Israeli Standard SI 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0, at level AA.

What we did on the site

  • The whole site can be navigated with a keyboard, with a clear marker showing where you are.
  • Page headings follow an orderly structure, so screen readers can jump between them.
  • Every form field has a label, and error and success messages are read out by screen readers.
  • The color contrast between text and background meets the standard.
  • The illustration on the home page has a text description, and purely decorative elements are hidden from screen readers.
  • Text can be enlarged in the browser without losing content.
  • The site is adapted to phones and computers.
  • Hebrew is shown right to left and English left to right.
  • Smooth scrolling turns off automatically for anyone who has set "reduce motion" on their device.

Known limitations

We are not currently aware of any accessibility limitations on the site.

Accessibility arrangements at our offices

D-Way does not currently have offices that receive visitors.
